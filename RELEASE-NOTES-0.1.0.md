## Pressfield 0.1.0

First public release.

Pressfield is a local-first macOS writing app where idle prose visibly decays
until typing resumes — a pressure field for drafting. Your text never leaves
your machine: storage is a local SQLite database.

- Signed with a Developer ID certificate, notarized by Apple, and stapled — the
  DMG opens cleanly on a stock Mac (macOS 26 era Gatekeeper).
- Apple Silicon (aarch64) build.

Integrity: the SHA-256 of the DMG and the full distribution receipt (build,
signing, notarization, stapling, and Gatekeeper evidence) are attached to this
release as `receipt-0.1.0.json`.
